Good fabric leaves
room for your story.

Bombay Fancy Cloth is a local fabric shop in Daska, Pakistan. We bring together unstitched suits for women and men, from the comfortable cottons you reach for every day to festive fabrics that make an occasion feel special.

There is something personal about choosing a print, feeling the fabric and picturing what it could become. We want our shop to make that discovery feel easy, welcoming and full of possibility.

Browse the collection online, then come find us at Machli Bazaar in Daska. Bring your ideas, your colour preferences and the name of your favourite tailor.
